4. When We Collect Your Information

4.1 Checkout

When placing an order, you provide identification, contact, and shipping information to generate the order and process payment via Stripe. The minimum information required to complete a purchase is: full name, email, phone number, and a complete US shipping address.

4.2 Account Registration

You may voluntarily create an account by providing an email, password, and full name. Phone number is optional.

4.3 Guest Order Tracking

Buyers without an account can check their order status at mrpapshop.com/us/seguimiento by entering their order code and the email used at purchase, which act as a two-factor check.

4.4 Email Communications

Mr. Paps may collect additional information when you contact us directly by email for support or other requests.

6. Cookies and Local Browser Storage

Mr. Paps uses browser local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) instead of third-party tracking cookies, for:

Shopping cart contents (product identifiers and prices)

Signed-in session tokens (JWT), for up to 30 days

Guest order tracking (order code and email), cleared when the browser tab is closed

You can configure your browser to block or clear local storage, though this may affect cart, login, and order-tracking functionality.

7. Data Retention

Completed order data: retained for the period required by applicable tax and commercial recordkeeping obligations

Cancelled or unpaid order data: 90 days, unless a dispute is pending

Active account data: retained while your account remains active or until you request deletion

Inactive account data: anonymized or deleted after 12 months of inactivity

Support and claims data: retained for 3 years after case resolution

Session tokens: 30 days

Once the applicable retention period ends, or when you exercise your deletion rights and no legal obligation requires retention, Mr. Paps will securely delete or irreversibly anonymize the data.

8. Security Measures

Password hashing using bcrypt with a high cost factor; no password is ever stored in plain text

JWT-based authentication signed with strong, independent secrets for customer and admin sessions

Thorough input validation on all API endpoints

Cryptographic verification (HMAC signature) of Stripe payment webhooks

Security headers: Content Security Policy (CSP), X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy

HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit

Admin panel access restricted to authorized accounts with independent credentials

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and Mr. Paps cannot guarantee absolute security.

Delete your account and associated personal information, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements (e.g., completed order records kept for tax purposes)
